# Wayreth: The Black Master v1.7.0

## Road to Wayreth campaign expansion

- Expands the campaign opening from Ispin Greenshield's funeral into a full 8–16 session overland arc.
- Campaign date: 18 Darkember, 351 AC. Night of the Eye: 1 Newkolt, 39 days later.
- Adds a master travel journal and three complete route journals.
- Route I: Great Solamnic Road — Maelgoth, Vingaard Keep, Solanthus, Thelgaard, Caergoth, Port O'Call, Crossing, Solace, Wayreth.
- Route II: Lemish Shortcut — southern Hinterlund, Throtl Gap, Lemish, Fangoth, Darkwoods, Restglen, Port O'Call, Crossing, Solace, Wayreth.
- Route III: Sea and Elven Road — Great Road to Caergoth, New Sea, Ankatavaka/Porliost, Qualinesti, Qualinost, Wayreth.
- Every stop contains arrival framing, named contacts, encounters, route knowledge, time-pressure consequences, and GM-only connective notes.
- Adds route-specific d12 encounter and intelligence tables plus optional adventures for expansion to sixteen sessions.
- Repositions Ossian Krell so he enters differently on each route while preserving Skaith, Mereth, the Forest refusal, Wayreth investigation, and Night of the Eye climax.
- Removes the old “Wayreth appears on any wooded road” shortcut from the opening premise. The party must physically make the journey.
- Adds a GM calendar-control button: **Road to Wayreth · 18 Darkember**, which sets the campaign date and lunar offsets so the next Night of the Eye is 1 Newkolt, 39 days later. It does not automatically overwrite an existing calendar.
- Preserves the original Wayreth CSS and journal styling.
- Total managed journal pages: 122.
