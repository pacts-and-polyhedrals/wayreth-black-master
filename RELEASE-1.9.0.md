# Wayreth: The Black Master — v1.9.0

## Corrected Three Roads to Wayreth

The Road to Wayreth arc has been rebuilt around the supplied marked Ansalon map. The previous route geography has been removed.

### Route I — The Sailor’s Road
**Vogler → Kalaman → Mirivale → Hylo → Gilthanost → Wayreth**

A port-and-sea campaign built around winter weather, continuous ship travel, crew relationships, Mirivale, Kenderhome, and Qualinesti access.

### Route II — The River and Heartland Road
**Vogler → Dargaard corridor → Maelgoth → Brasdel → Solanthus → Garnet → Gilthanost or Zaradene → Wayreth**

The most adaptable route, with roads, river traffic, replacement horses, Solamnic cities, Dargaard pressure, and a genuine transport branch at Garnet.

### Route III — The Wild Road
**Vogler → Cericas → Fangoth → Solace → Haven → Pax Tharkas → Wayreth**

The direct adventurer’s route, with the highest wilderness and political risk, Fangoth as a major playable location, and a classic Dragonlance journey through Solace, Haven, and Pax Tharkas.

## Expanded journal material

- The corrected route map is now included directly in the module as Ispin’s handout.
- The Road guide has been rewritten around the three correct routes and a 26 Frostkolt practical arrival deadline.
- Each route contains a complete itinerary, every major stop, route-specific NPCs, scenes, knowledge, time consequences, d12 encounter and rumor tables, transport/survival procedures, Krell/Skaith/Mereth integration, signature adventures, optional expansion adventures, and a route finale.
- AI/meta commentary and external source-link prose have been removed from the in-game journals. The journals now read as GM campaign material and in-world Ispin notes.
- Existing Session 0, spells, Staff of Magius, magic items, Wayreth styling, and calendar systems remain intact.
