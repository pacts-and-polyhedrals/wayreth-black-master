## Cantrip spells

### Guiding Sigil

**Divination · Cantrip · White Robe · Wayreth original**

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Target:** One object
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 hour
- **Access:** Order spell

You touch an object no larger than 5 feet in any dimension and place a faint silver sigil upon it for 1 hour. While the object is within 300 feet of you and on the same plane, you know the direction to it. You can maintain only one guiding sigil at a time; casting this spell again ends the previous sigil.

### Silver Ward

**Abjuration · Cantrip · White Robe · Wayreth original**

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** Self
- **Target:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 round
- **Access:** Order spell

You trace a silver crescent before yourself. Until the start of your next turn, you gain a +1 bonus to AC. The first time before then that you make a saving throw against a spell or magical effect, you may roll a d4 and add it to the save after seeing the d20 but before the outcome is declared. **Enchantment · Cantrip · White Robe · Wayreth original**

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Target:** One willing creature
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

**Divination · 1st-level · White Robe · Legacy conversion**

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** Self (60-foot cone)
- **Target:** 60-foot cone
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes
- **Access:** Order spell

For the duration, you sense magical bonds between creatures in a 60-foot cone and familiars, bonded companions, magically summoned steeds, or similar creatures bound to another being. ### Cleaning

**Transmutation · Cantrip · Red Robe · Legacy conversion**

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Target:** One 20-foot cube
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Access:** Order spell

You cleanse a 20-foot cube within range. Choose any number of the following, in any combination, as part of the same casting:

- Remove dirt, grime, soot, dust, ash, cobwebs, mud, blood, and similar filth from surfaces, objects, and creatures. A creature that is unwilling is unaffected.

- Dry anything soaked, and clear standing water up to an inch deep, without harming living plants.

- Remove one nonmagical stain, tarnish, rust, or corrosion, however old and however set. This restores appearance, not structural damage.

- Suppress all odour in the area for 1 hour, or replace it with any ordinary scent you have smelled before.

- Purify enough food and drink for ten creatures, as purify food and drink.

- Launder and press worn clothing, restoring it to a fresh state.

The spell does not remove disease, curses, poison already in a creature, or magical marks; it does not erase deliberate writing, paint, dye, or tattoo; and it does not repair damage. Traces destroyed by this spell cannot be recovered by ordinary investigation, though divination and similar magic may still find them.

Red Robes are taught this in their first tenday and use it for the rest of their lives. It is not a small spell; it is a spell whose usefulness is unglamorous, which the Order regards as a distinction worth teaching early.

The recipient regains one expended spell slot of the same level or lower. A creature cannot lose or regain a spell slot from transfer essence again until it finishes a long rest. The spell fails if the donor has no suitable unexpended slot or the recipient has no suitable expended slot.

## 6th-level spells

### Death's Due

**Necromancy · 6th-level · Black Robe · Wayreth original**

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Target:** One creature
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
- **Access:** Order spell

Choose one creature you can see within range. It must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it is marked for the duration. The first time on each round that the marked creature takes damage, it takes an additional 4d8 necrotic damage and you regain hit points equal to half the extra damage dealt. This costs you nothing on your turn or off it. The target repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the spell on a success.

### Raistlin's Wheel of Flame

**Evocation · 6th-level · Black Robe · Legacy conversion**

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Target:** 30-foot-radius, 20-foot-high cylinder
- **Components:** V, S, M (a fire opal set in a gold ring worth 250 gp, not consumed)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
- **Access:** Order spell

A stationary wheel of multihued flame blooms in a 30-foot-radius, 20-foot-high cylinder centered on a point you can see. When a creature other than you enters the area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it makes a Dexterity saving throw. It takes 6d6 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success. At the start of each of your turns while you maintain concentration, the damage increases by 1d6, to a maximum of 12d6. You are immune to the wheel's damage. A creature can take this damage only once per turn.

### Silence the Defiant

**Enchantment · 6th-level · Black Robe · Wayreth original**

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Target:** Up to six creatures
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
- **Access:** Order spell

Choose up to six creatures you can see within range. Each must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, a creature cannot speak above a whisper and cannot provide verbal components for spells for the duration. An affected creature repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns on which it has taken damage, ending the effect on itself on a success. A creature that cannot speak is unaffected.

## 7th-level spells

### Edict of the Black Moon

**Enchantment · 7th-level · Black Robe · Wayreth original**

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Target:** Up to eight creatures
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute
- **Access:** Order spell

Choose up to eight creatures you can see within range and give one command of no more than five words. Each target makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it must use its action on each of its turns to obey the command as completely as it can, for the duration. At the end of each of its turns it repeats the save, ending the effect on a success. As a bonus action on your turn you may replace the command with a different one of five words or fewer; each creature still affected repeats the saving throw when you do. A target automatically succeeds on any save made immediately before obeying a command that would directly kill or permanently maim it. The spell ends on a target when it succeeds on a save.

### Kiss of Night's Guardian

**Necromancy · 7th-level · Black Robe · Legacy conversion**

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Target:** One living creature
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Up to 24 hours; special
- **Access:** Tower Master secret

You touch one living creature and mark its brow with an invisible seal recognized by the undead guardians of the Shoikan Grove. For 24 hours, the creature has advantage on saving throws against fear caused by undead. Within the Shoikan Grove, undead bound to the Grove treat the bearer as protected by the Master and will not willingly attack it unless it attacks them first or the Master revokes the protection. The spell ends after the bearer completes one passage through the Grove, entering or leaving.

### Soul Chain

**Necromancy · 7th-level · Black Robe · Wayreth original**

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Target:** One creature
- **Components:** V, S, M (a black sapphire worth 1,000 gp, not consumed unless it traps a soul; then consumed when the gem ends)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute; soul gem up to 24 hours
- **Access:** Order spell

One creature you can see within range must make a Charisma saving throw. On a failed save it takes 9d8 necrotic damage and a black chain visible only in magical light binds its spirit for the duration; while bound, it cannot regain hit points. On a successful save it takes half as much damage and is not bound. If it dies while bound, its soul is trapped in a black gem that appears in your hand, provided you are on the same plane. While trapped, the creature cannot be returned to life. The gem lasts for 24 hours, until destroyed (AC 20, 20 hit points, immune poison and psychic), or until you release the soul as an action, after which normal resurrection is possible.

## 8th-level spells

**Necromancy · 8th-level · Black Robe · Wayreth original**

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Target:** One creature
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous; memory loss until long rest
- **Access:** Order spell

### Sable Cataclysm

**Evocation · 8th-level · Black Robe · Wayreth original**

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 150 feet
- **Target:** 30-foot-radius sphere
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous; darkness until end of your next turn
- **Access:** Order spell

A sphere of collapsing black fire erupts at a point you can see within range. ## 9th-level spells

### Final Dominion

**Enchantment · 9th-level · Black Robe · Wayreth original**

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Target:** One creature
- **Components:** V, S, M (a black crown-shaped onyx worth 2,500 gp, not consumed)
- **Duration:** 24 hours
- **Access:** Order spell

One creature you can see within range must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, it is charmed by you for 24 hours without concentration. While charmed, it regards your spoken commands as overriding imperatives and obeys them on its turn to the best of its ability. Whenever you order it to take an action that would obviously cause its own death or permanent mutilation, it repeats the saving throw before acting, ending the spell on a success. Whenever it takes damage from you or a creature it knows to be your ally, it also repeats the saving throw. A creature that succeeds on any save against final dominion is immune to this spell from you for 30 days.

### Reverse Death

**Necromancy · 9th-level · Black Robe · Legacy conversion**

- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Target:** One corpse dead no longer than 10 days
- **Components:** V, S, M (a black pearl worth 5,000 gp, consumed)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous
- **Access:** Order spell

You touch the corpse of a creature that has been dead no longer than 10 days and force its soul back into the body. A soul that is free and willing returns at once; an unwilling or reluctant soul must succeed on a Charisma saving throw against your spell save DC or be compelled to return anyway. The creature returns to life with 10d8 + your spellcasting ability modifier hit points and two levels of exhaustion, and any poison or nonmagical disease afflicting it at death is neutralised. This spell does not restore missing body parts, lift a curse, or remove a condition that caused the death; if the body cannot survive, the creature may die again. The black pearl is consumed. A creature returned by reverse death cannot be targeted by it again for 30 days. Unlike divine resurrection, this working leaves a detectable necromantic scar on the soul until the creature next dies.
